RACE MODULE — 5K / 10K
======================

WHAT THE RACE DEMANDS
---------------------
5K and 10K are run at or above lactate threshold with a heavy VO2max component.
Priorities, in order: aerobic base (the foundation everything sits on), VO2max
development (the primary race-specific stimulus), lactate threshold, speed and
running economy, race-pace pacing skill. Speed without base collapses; base
without sharpening leaves time on the table.

DISTANCE-SPECIFIC STRUCTURE
---------------------------
- Typical week: 1–2 quality sessions, one long run, easy volume, strides 2–3×/week.
- Long runs are 25–35% of weekly volume — typically peaking 12–18 km. They build
  aerobic durability, not race-specific fatigue resistance; easy pace only, and
  they must stay structurally meaningful (never shorter than a typical easy run,
  even late in the plan).
- Quality tiers by current volume: under 30 km/week → one quality session, 80%+
  easy, strides over structured speed. 30–50 km/week → up to two quality sessions.
  Over 50 km/week with 8+ weeks of consistency and clean recovery → a third
  session is available; if used, justify it in the overview.
- VO2max intervals: 2–5 min reps (800–1600 m) at 3K–5K effort with roughly equal
  recovery. Repetition work: 200–400 m fast-and-relaxed with full recovery.
  Race-pace blocks of 2–4 km belong in the final 3–4 weeks.
- Define both 5K and 10K race pace in the table, but train only the goal-distance
  race pace; the other is reference.
- Strength emphasis for short-distance athletes: power, plyometric readiness,
  running economy — best stacked after interval or threshold days.

TAPER
-----
Short and sharp: 7–10 days for 5K, 10–14 days for 10K. Cut volume 40–50% in the
final week, keep 1–2 short sharp touches (last one 2–3 days out: short intervals
or strides), stay light and energized. Beginners detrain fast — never give them
two consecutive low-volume weeks; keep 3–5 km easy runs through the final 10 days.

Derivation anchors: 5K race pace = goal 5K time ÷ 5; 10K pace ≈ 5K pace + 10–15
sec/km; interval ≈ 5K pace or slightly faster; threshold ≈ 10K pace + 10–15 sec/km;
easy ≈ threshold + 60–90 sec/km. Bias training paces toward demonstrated fitness
when the goal is ahead of it.
