RACE MODULE — HALF MARATHON
===========================

WHAT THE RACE DEMANDS
---------------------
The half marathon is run at or near lactate threshold for 75–120 minutes — the
sustained-discomfort zone. The athlete needs, in order: lactate threshold (the
primary engine — without it pace falls apart after km 10), aerobic capacity (the
foundation that makes threshold work absorbable), fatigue resistance (holding form
through the final 5 km), and a modest speed reserve as multiplier. Intensity
distribution for most athletes: roughly 80% easy, 15% threshold, 5% faster work.

DISTANCE-SPECIFIC STRUCTURE
---------------------------
- Threshold work is the centerpiece of the build: continuous tempos (15–35 min),
  cruise intervals, wave tempos, progression tempos. Rotate variants to keep the
  stimulus fresh without changing its purpose.
- Race-pace tempo blocks (4–8 km at goal pace inside an easy run) belong in the
  peak phase only, after the threshold base exists, replacing — not adding to — a
  quality session that week.
- VO2max intervals are a late-build/peak tool for intermediate/advanced athletes,
  substituting for one threshold session.
- A midweek medium-long run (10–14 km easy) partners the long run for cumulative
  fatigue resistance.
- Long runs peak at 18–20 km (22 km rarely, experienced athletes only); several
  16–18 km runs beat one maximal effort. Floors once the plan is established:
  ≥12 km after week 4, ≥14 km after week 6, recovery weeks included. Progression
  finishes and tempo finishes are peak-phase quality sessions and count as such.
- Strength emphasis: hip stability, glute strength, single-leg power, core
  endurance for late-race form.

TAPER
-----
10–14 days. Week before race: volume down 30–40%, one short sharp session
(e.g. 2–3 × 1 km at threshold), medium-long run suspended. Race week: a few short
easy runs, strides two days out, 20–30 min shakeout the day before. The athlete
should feel restless and eager — that is correct.

Derivation anchors: HMP = goal time ÷ 21.0975 (locked to goal); threshold ≈ HMP −
10–20 sec/km; easy ≈ HMP + 60–90 sec/km; recovery ≈ easy + 20–30 sec/km; steady
between easy and HMP; interval ≈ threshold − 15–25 sec/km; repetition ≈ interval −
10–15 sec/km.
