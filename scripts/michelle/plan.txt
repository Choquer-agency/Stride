COACHING OVERVIEW
Three weeks ago the question was whether you would start; this week you ran three times and finished on a 5 km. That changes the plan, and it changes the target: the Maui Half Marathon on Sunday, January 17, 2027, alongside Bryce — 18 weeks from the week you just ran. The goal is unchanged and it is the only goal: run the entire 21.1 km without a walk break, no time in mind. Every week has three runs — two shorter ones and a long run that grows by roughly a kilometre a week, with a down week every fourth week so your tendons and joints catch up with your Peloton-built engine — plus one Peloton Strength class, which replaces the rides from the old plan. Each session is placed on a suggested day, but with a three-month-old at home the days are yours: do any of this week's sessions on any day, in any order, back to back if that is what the week allows. The only rule is that the week closes Sunday night — nothing gets carried into Monday, and nothing from next week gets pulled forward. If a week only has room for three sessions, the runs come first and the strength class is the one to drop. Pace guides are rough and ease slightly as you get fitter; conversational effort always wins over the number. Any pain that changes how you walk or is still there the next morning means rest that day — never run through it, and never squeeze a missed week into the next one.

EFFORT TABLE:
Easy Run: 9:00–9:45/km early, easing to 8:30–9:15/km by race season — conversational, Zone 2 heart rate, RPE 3–4
Long Run: same effort, sit at the easier end of your current range
Peloton Strength: any 20–30 min Peloton strength class — lower body or full body, your pick
