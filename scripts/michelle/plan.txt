COACHING OVERVIEW
You arrive with a real aerobic engine from consistent Peloton work — what you don't have yet is running-specific durability: tendons, joints, and the ability to hold a conversational jog without stopping, and that is your primary limiter. This plan attacks it with a 35-week runway that no shortcut can match: run/walk progressions through week 12, your first 30-minute continuous run in week 13 — well before halfway — then a patient continuous-running build that takes your long run past two hours before race week. Running the full 21.1 km without a single walk break is an achievable goal on this timeline, provided you respect the boring weeks as much as the milestone weeks. Every session lives on Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday as committed; Thursdays are Peloton Zone 2 rides for the first six weeks to let your legs adapt before the third run day arrives in week 7, and from week 25 a short fourth session appears on Wednesdays because at your peak long runs it materially protects the goal. One rule is non-negotiable: any pain that changes how you walk or is still there the next morning means walk-only or rest that day — never run through it, and never compress a missed week into the next one. Monday stays yours: yoga, and a hard rest day from running.

EFFORT TABLE:
Walk: brisk but comfortable, RPE 2
Jog: conversational — you could speak a full sentence, RPE 3–4
Ride (Zone 2): steady, could chat the whole ride, RPE 3–4
